About

Jesse Hudson, a native of Las Vegas, Nevada, is a mixed media artist whose work reflects his profound exploration of innovation, technology, and human behavior. Drawing inspiration from his personal experiences, family dynamics, and significant historical and contemporary events, he creates captivating pieces that bring his visionary concepts to life. Hudson employs a range of techniques, including prop, scenic, and costume fabrication, to craft breathtaking installations, which he skillfully captures through his camera before deconstruction. In addition to his mastery of installation art, Hudson boasts a wealth of experience in commercial photography, specializing in music, special events, culinary delights, cocktails, and product imagery.
