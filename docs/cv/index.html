Education: 


Standard Diploma, Photography

Arbor View High school I 2011-2016


Hudson attended Arbor View High School, where he dedicated four years to studying photography as his chosen elective. During this time, he achieved notable accolades, including a perfect score of 5 on his senior AP Portfolio. His accomplishments also include earning the Scholastic Gold Key for Visual Arts, securing 1st place in 3D Sculpture at the College of Southern Nevada Voice Art Competition, and being honored with the Best Senior Show award, among others.

• Curating a diverse collection of film cameras, art books, and vintage photography essentials to meet the needs of enthusiasts and collectors.

• Providing professional film development services with an unwavering commitment to quality and precision.
• Designing and managing our visually striking storefront in downtown Las Vegas, ensuring it reflects our brand’s artistic vision.
• Coordinating pop-up booths, installations, and art shows at local festivals to expand our reach and engage with the creative community.
• Organizing and curating art exhibitions that celebrate visual storytelling and support local artists.
